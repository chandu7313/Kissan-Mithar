## 2. Key Governance Decisions for Founder Sign-Off

### Decision 1: Exclusion of Chairman's Casting Vote
- **The Issue**: Under default Table F Regulation 68(ii), the Chairman has a second/casting vote in case of equality of votes. If granted to Ranjith as Chairman, Ranjith would have unilateral control over all Board decisions during any disagreement.
- **Our Recommendation**: **Exclude the casting vote completely**. In a 2-director board, both founders should govern by consensus.
- **Founder Selection**:
  - [x] **APPROVE**: Exclude casting vote at both Board and General Meeting levels (Recommended).
  - [ ] **ALTERNATIVE**: Grant casting vote to Chairperson on routine operational matters only.

---

### Decision 2: Multi-Tier Board Deadlock Protocol
- **The Issue**: In a 2-director board with no casting vote, a 1-1 tie creates a deadlock. Without an internal resolution procedure, deadlocks can force company shutdown or court litigation.
- **Our Recommendation**: Adopt a 4-tier escalation mechanism:
  1. *Tier 1*: 14-day informal consultation between Ranjith and Chandra Mohan.
  2. *Tier 2*: 21-day mediation by a mutually agreed senior advocate, CA, or CS.
  3. *Tier 3*: Binding expert determination on accounting, valuation, or software IP issues.
  4. *Tier 4*: Shareholder referral or voluntary buyout under a separate Shareholders' Agreement (SHA).
- **Founder Selection**:
  - [x] **APPROVE**: Adopt the 4-tier deadlock resolution protocol in Article 36 (Recommended).
  - [ ] **ALTERNATIVE**: Modify escalation timelines or refer directly to arbitration.

---

### Decision 3: Right of First Refusal (ROFR) & Fair Valuation Mechanism
- **The Issue**: How to handle a founder wishing to sell shares to an outsider.
- **Our Recommendation**:
  - 30-day Right of First Refusal giving the non-selling founder the right to purchase the offered shares pro-rata.
  - Valuation in case of dispute or transmission determined by an **IBBI-Registered Valuer** or independent Practising Chartered Accountant using the Discounted Free Cash Flow (DCF) or Net Asset Value (NAV) method under Rule 11UA.
  - Carve-out for Permitted Transfers (transfer to immediate family or 100% owned holding company, retaining voting control).
- **Founder Selection**:
  - [x] **APPROVE**: Adopt 30-day ROFR, IBBI fair valuation, and family permitted transfers in Articles 16–20 (Recommended).
  - [ ] **ALTERNATIVE**: Require 100% board consent for any transfer with no permitted transfers.

---

### Decision 4: Reserved Matters (Affirmative Voting Rights) & Financial Thresholds
- **The Issue**: Protecting the 45% founder against unilateral majority decisions by the 55% founder on critical company actions.
- **Our Recommendation**: Adopt 12 Reserved Matters requiring the affirmative written consent of **both Founder Directors** (MOA/AOA changes, capital issues, ESOP, IP transfers, M&A, related-party contracts, winding up).
- **Borrowing / Asset Disposal Threshold**: Proposed at **INR 5,00,000** (Indian Rupees Five Lakhs) initially.
- **Founder Selection**:
  - [x] **APPROVE**: Adopt 12 Reserved Matters with INR 5,00,000 financial threshold (Recommended).
  - [ ] **ALTERNATIVE**: Adjust financial threshold to: INR ______________.

---

### Decision 5: Statutory Entrenchment under Section 5(3) & 5(4)
- **The Issue**: Ensuring that vital founder rights (ROFR, Deadlock, Reserved Matters) cannot be diluted or modified in the future by an ordinary or special resolution.
- **Our Recommendation**: Entrench Articles 16, 17, 36, 41, and 42 in Form SPICe+ Part B and INC-34, requiring **100% unanimous written consent of all members** to amend.
- **Founder Selection**:
  - [x] **APPROVE**: Formally entrench Articles 16, 17, 36, 41, 42 under Section 5(3) (Recommended).
  - [ ] **ALTERNATIVE**: Do not entrench; rely on standard special resolution (75%).

---

### Decision 6: Pre-Emptive Rights & Future Dilution (Section 62)
- **The Issue**: Ensuring founders can participate pro-rata in future funding rounds to prevent involuntary dilution.
- **Our Recommendation**: Strict pro-rata pre-emptive rights for any further issue of shares or convertible instruments under Section 62(1)(a), with standard exceptions for approved ESOP pools (up to 10%) or strategic allotments approved by both founders.
- **Founder Selection**:
  - [x] **APPROVE**: Adopt pre-emptive rights in Article 5 (Recommended).
  - [ ] **ALTERNATIVE**: Allow Board discretion on private placements without pre-emption.

---

### Decision 7: Founder Exit, Departure & Transmission Call Option
- **The Issue**: What happens if a founder dies, becomes incapacitated, or leaves the company.
- **Our Recommendation**:
  - *Transmission*: Shares transmit to legal heirs under Section 56. The surviving founder has a **Call Option** exercisable within 180 days to purchase the transmitted shares at Fair Market Value determined by a Registered Valuer.
  - *Operational Departure / Leaver Rules*: Keep detailed good-leaver / bad-leaver share buyback formulas, equity vesting schedules, and non-compete covenants inside a **separate confidential Shareholders' Agreement (SHA)** rather than the public AOA.
- **Founder Selection**:
  - [x] **APPROVE**: Adopt 180-day transmission call option in Article 22; reserve vesting/non-compete for SHA (Recommended).
  - [ ] **ALTERNATIVE**: Embed leaver and vesting clauses directly in the AOA.

---

### Decision 8: Tag-Along and Drag-Along Rights
- **The Issue**: Whether to include tag-along (minority co-sale right) and drag-along (majority drag right on 100% company sale) in the AOA.
- **Our Recommendation**: Tag-along and drag-along provisions are complex commercial mechanisms that belong in a **Shareholders' Agreement (SHA)**, tailored when the first institutional angel/seed investor enters. Including generic drag-along clauses in the initial incorporation AOA creates risk of inadvertent founder oppression.
- **Founder Selection**:
  - [x] **APPROVE**: Defer drag-along/tag-along to future SHA / Series A articles (Recommended).
  - [ ] **ALTERNATIVE**: Draft basic tag-along protection for 45% founder in AOA.

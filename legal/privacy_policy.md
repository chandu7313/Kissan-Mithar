# Privacy Policy — Kissan Mithar

**Last Updated: September 5, 2026**

Kissan Mithar ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of Indian farmers and agricultural professionals who use our mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide
- **Phone Number** — Used for OTP-based login and account creation.
- **Name** — Your display name within the app.
- **Location Data** (Village, District, State) — Provided during farm profile setup.
- **Farm Details** — Land size (acres), soil type, water sources, existing crops, and electricity availability.
- **Farm Photos & Survey Maps** — Images of your land uploaded for orchard planning.
- **Voice Notes** — Optional audio recordings submitted with orchard planning requests.
- **Language Preference** — Your chosen language (English, Hindi, Telugu, or Kannada).

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **GPS Coordinates** — Collected with your explicit permission to map your farm location accurately.
- **Device Information** — Device type, platform (Android/iOS), and FCM token for push notifications.
- **Usage Data** — App interaction logs for improving our service.

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Weather Data** — We fetch weather forecasts from OpenWeatherMap using your GPS location.
- **Mandi Prices** — Market price data sourced from government agricultural databases.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---------|-----------|
| Account creation & login | Phone number, name |
| Orchard planning reports | Farm photos, GPS, land details, preferences |
| Weather alerts | GPS coordinates |
| Market price notifications | District, state, crop preferences |
| Expert consultations | Farm details, voice notes, photos |
| Push notifications | FCM device token |
| App improvement | Usage analytics (anonymized) |

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- Your data is stored securely on **Supabase (PostgreSQL)** hosted in Australia (ap-southeast-2).
- Farm photos and media are stored on **Cloudinary** with encrypted transport.
- All API communications use **HTTPS/TLS encryption**.
- Authentication tokens (JWT) are stored securely on your device.
- Passwords (for expert/admin accounts) are hashed using **bcrypt**.

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We do **NOT** sell your personal data. We share data only with:

| Third Party | Purpose | Data Shared |
|-------------|---------|-------------|
| Firebase (Google) | Push notifications, authentication | Device token, phone number |
| Cloudinary | Image storage | Farm photos |
| OpenWeatherMap | Weather data | GPS coordinates (lat/long only) |
| Fast2SMS | OTP delivery | Phone number |

---

## 5. Your Rights (Under DPDP Act, 2023)

As per India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

- **Access** — Request a copy of your personal data.
- **Correction** — Update or correct inaccurate data.
- **Erasure** — Request deletion of your account and associated data.
- **Grievance Redressal** — File complaints about data handling.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: **privacy@kissanmithar.in**

---

## 6. Data Retention

- Active accounts: Data retained as long as your account is active.
- Deleted accounts: Personal data erased within 30 days of deletion request.
- Authentication audit logs: Retained for 90 days for security purposes.
- Expired OTPs: Automatically deleted within 1 hour of expiry.

Kissan Mithar is not intended for use by children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

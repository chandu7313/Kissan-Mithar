# CHAPTER IV: COMPANY'S LIEN

### Article 11: Paramount Lien on Shares and Dividends
11.1 The Company shall have a first and paramount lien:
- (a) on every share (not being a fully paid share), for all monies (whether presently payable or not) called, or payable at a fixed time, in respect of that share; and
- (b) on all shares (not being fully paid shares) standing registered in the name of a single person, for all monies presently payable by him or his estate to the Company.  
11.2 The Company's lien, if any, on a share shall extend to all dividends or distributions declared or payable in respect thereof.  
11.3 The Board may at any time declare any share to be wholly or in part exempt from the provisions of this Article.

### Article 12: Enforcement of Lien by Sale and Proceeds Application
12.1 The Company may sell, in such manner as the Board thinks fit, any shares on which the Company has a lien, but no sale shall be made unless a sum in respect of which the lien exists is presently payable and a notice in writing stating and demanding payment of such sum has been given to the registered holder for the time being of the share or the person entitled thereto by reason of his death or insolvency, and default has been made in payment for fourteen (14) days after such notice.  
12.2 The net proceeds of the sale shall be applied in payment of such part of the amount in respect of which the lien exists as is presently payable, and the residue, if any, shall (subject to a like lien for sums not presently payable as existed upon the shares before the sale) be paid to the person entitled to the shares at the date of the sale.

# CHAPTER V: CALLS ON SHARES

### Article 13: Board's Power to Make Calls
13.1 The Board may, from time to time, make calls upon the members in respect of any monies unpaid on their shares (whether on account of the nominal value of the shares or by way of premium) and not by the conditions of allotment thereof made payable at fixed times.  
13.2 Each call shall not exceed one-fourth (25%) of the nominal value of the share or be payable at less than one month from the date fixed for the payment of the last preceding call.  
13.3 Each member shall receive at least thirty (30) days' notice specifying the time and place of payment and shall pay to the Company, at the time and place so specified, the amount called on his shares.

### Article 14: Payment of Calls and Interest on Overdue Calls
14.1 The joint holders of a share shall be jointly and severally liable to pay all calls in respect thereof.  
14.2 If a sum called in respect of a share is not paid before or on the day appointed for payment thereof, the person from whom the sum is due shall pay interest upon the sum at the rate of ten percent (10%) per annum from the day appointed for the payment thereof to the time of actual payment, or at such lower rate, if any, as the Board may determine. The Board shall be at liberty to waive payment of any such interest wholly or in part.

### Article 15: Advance Payment of Calls
15.1 The Board may, if it thinks fit, receive from any member willing to advance the same, all or any part of the monies uncalled and unpaid upon any shares held by him.  
15.2 Upon all or any of the monies so advanced, the Company may pay interest at such rate not exceeding twelve percent (12%) per annum as may be agreed upon between the Board and the member paying the sum in advance; provided that such advance payments shall not confer any right to participate in profits or dividends or confer any additional voting rights prior to the due date of call.

# CHAPTER VI: TRANSFER OF SHARES & FOUNDER PROTECTION

### Article 16: Mandatory Restrictions on Share Transfer
16.1 The Company is a private company, and accordingly, the right to transfer shares in the Company is strictly restricted as provided in this Chapter.  
16.2 No Member shall sell, transfer, assign, pledge, charge, encumber, or otherwise dispose of any legal or beneficial interest in any share of the Company, except in strict accordance with the provisions of Article 17 (Right of First Refusal), Article 18 (Permitted Transfers), or Article 22 (Transmission Call Option). Any transfer or encumbrance attempted in violation of this Chapter shall be null and void ab initio, and the Company shall not register or recognize any such purported transfer.  
16.3 Every transfer of shares shall be effected by an instrument of transfer in writing in **Form SH-4**, in accordance with Section 56 of the Act, duly stamped, dated, and executed by or on behalf of the transferor and transferee, and accompanied by the certificate of the shares to which it relates.

### Article 17: Right of First Refusal (ROFR) Mechanics
17.1 Except in the case of a Permitted Transfer under Article 18, if any Member ("Selling Member") desires to sell, transfer, or dispose of all or any of his shares ("Offered Shares") to any third party, such Selling Member shall first offer the Offered Shares to the other existing Members (and specifically to the other Founder) in accordance with the procedures set forth herein.  
17.2 The Selling Member shall deliver a written notice (**"Transfer Notice"**) to the Board of Directors and to all other Members ("Non-Selling Members"), specifying:
- (a) the number of Offered Shares;
- (b) the proposed sale price per share (**"Offer Price"**) and proposed payment terms;
- (c) the bona fide identity, registered address, and beneficial ownership of the proposed third-party purchaser; and
- (d) a copy of the binding written offer or term sheet received from the third-party purchaser.

17.3 The Non-Selling Members shall have a period of **thirty (30) calendar days** from the date of receipt of the Transfer Notice (**"ROFR Offer Period"**) to exercise their right to purchase all or any part of the Offered Shares at the Offer Price (or at the Fair Market Value determined pursuant to Article 19, if the Non-Selling Member disputes that the Offer Price reflects fair value).  
17.4 If more than one Non-Selling Member exercises the right to purchase the Offered Shares, the Offered Shares shall be allocated among such exercising Members pro-rata to their existing equity shareholding in the Company.  
17.5 If the Non-Selling Members elect to purchase all of the Offered Shares, the closing of the sale and purchase shall take place within thirty (30) days from the expiry of the ROFR Offer Period against payment of the purchase price.  
17.6 If the Non-Selling Members do not elect to purchase all of the Offered Shares within the ROFR Offer Period, the Selling Member may, within a period of **sixty (60) calendar days** following the expiry of the ROFR Offer Period, transfer the remaining Offered Shares to the designated third-party purchaser identified in the Transfer Notice, strictly at a price not less than the Offer Price and on terms no more favourable to the purchaser than those specified in the Transfer Notice. If the transfer is not completed within such 60-day period, the restrictions of this Article shall re-apply in full.

### Article 18: Permitted Transfers
18.1 Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in Article 17, any Founder or Member may at any time transfer all or part of his shares to:
- (a) the spouse, children, parents, or lineal descendants of such Founder or Member; or
- (b) a private family trust established solely for the benefit of such Founder or his immediate family members; or
- (c) a body corporate or holding company wherein one hundred percent (100%) of the equity capital and voting control is beneficially held and continuously retained by such Founder;  
(each such transfer being a **"Permitted Transfer"** and each such transferee being a **"Permitted Transferee"**).  
18.2 A Permitted Transfer shall be exempt from the Right of First Refusal under Article 17, provided always that:
- (a) the transferring Founder must retain all voting rights and management powers associated with such shares by executing an irrevocable power of attorney or proxy in favour of himself; and
- (b) the Permitted Transferee executes an undertaking agreeing to be bound by all the provisions of these Articles; and
- (c) if at any time the Permitted Transferee ceases to satisfy the qualifications of a Permitted Transferee, such transferee shall immediately re-transfer the shares back to the transferring Founder.

### Article 19: Fair Market Valuation Mechanism
19.1 Where these Articles provide for the determination of the **Fair Market Value (FMV)** of shares (including in connection with ROFR disputes, transmission call options, or leaver buyouts), the Fair Market Value shall be determined by an **Independent Registered Valuer** registered under Section 247 of the Act with the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI), or an independent practising Chartered Accountant of repute mutually agreed upon by the Founders.  
19.2 If the Founders are unable to agree upon a valuer within fourteen (14) days, the statutory auditor of the Company shall act as the valuer, or if the auditor declines, an independent registered valuer shall be nominated by the President of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).  
19.3 The valuer shall determine the fair value of the shares as a going concern on a stand-alone basis, using internationally accepted valuation methodologies (including the Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) or Net Asset Value (NAV) method, as appropriate under Rule 11UA of the Income-tax Rules, 1962), without applying any minority discount or illiquidity discount to the founder's shares.  
19.4 The valuer shall act as an **expert and not as an arbitrator**. The determination of the valuer shall be final and binding on all parties in the absence of manifest error or fraud. The fees and expenses of the valuer shall be shared equally between the transferor and transferee.

### Article 20: Board's Power to Refuse Registration of Transfer
20.1 The Board of Directors may, subject to the provisions of Section 58 of the Act, refuse to register the transfer of any shares in the Company if:
- (a) the transfer is in contravention of the restrictions contained in Article 16, Article 17, Article 18, or Article 41;
- (b) the instrument of transfer is not duly stamped, dated, or executed, or is not accompanied by the relevant share certificate;
- (c) the transfer would result in the number of members of the Company exceeding the statutory limit of two hundred (200);
- (d) the transferee is a minor, person of unsound mind, or insolvent (save in case of transmission to legal guardian);
- (e) the transferee is a direct commercial competitor of the Company in agriculture, agritech, or agricultural e-commerce; or
- (f) the Company has a paramount lien on the shares under Article 11.

20.2 If the Board refuses to register the transfer of any shares, the Company shall, within **thirty (30) days** from the date on which the instrument of transfer was lodged with the Company, send notice of the refusal to the transferor and the transferee, stating the specific, lawful reasons for such refusal pursuant to Section 58(2) and Section 58(3) of the Act.

# CHAPTER VII: TRANSMISSION & FORFEITURE OF SHARES

### Article 21: Transmission of Shares on Death, Lunacy or Insolvency
21.1 On the death of a member, the survivor or survivors where the member was a joint holder, and his legal representatives or lawful nominee appointed under Section 72 of the Act where he was a sole holder, shall be the only persons recognized by the Company as having any title to his interest in the shares.  
21.2 Any person becoming entitled to a share in consequence of the death or insolvency of a member may, upon such evidence being produced as may from time to time properly be required by the Board, and subject to Article 22, elect either:
- (a) to be registered himself as holder of the share; or
- (b) to make such transfer of the share as the deceased or insolvent member could have made.  
21.3 The provisions of these Articles relating to the right to transfer and the registration of transfers of shares shall apply to any such notice or transfer as aforesaid as if the death or insolvency of the member had not occurred.

### Article 22: Founder Continuity & Transmission Call Option
22.1 In order to safeguard the operational continuity, agronomic technology leadership, and commercial stability of the Company in the unfortunate event of the death, permanent legal incapacity, or bankruptcy of either Founder ("Departing Founder"):
- (a) The shares held by the Departing Founder shall transmit to his lawful legal heirs or nominees in accordance with Section 56 of the Act.
- (b) In consideration of the personal-service and founder-led nature of the Company, the surviving Founder ("Surviving Founder") shall have the irrevocable right and option (**"Transmission Call Option"**), exercisable within **one hundred and eighty (180) calendar days** from the date of formal transmission, to purchase up to one hundred percent (100%) of the transmitted shares from the legal heirs or estate of the Departing Founder.
- (c) The purchase price payable by the Surviving Founder upon exercise of the Transmission Call Option shall be the **Fair Market Value** per share determined by an Independent Registered Valuer pursuant to Article 19.
- (d) The purchase price may be paid by the Surviving Founder in up to four (4) equal quarterly instalments over a period of twelve (12) months from the date of the valuation report, carrying simple interest at the prevailing State Bank of India Marginal Cost of Funds Based Lending Rate (MCLR) on the outstanding balance.
- (e) Upon payment of each instalment, the legal heirs shall execute all requisite Form SH-4 transfer deeds and surrender share certificates.

### Article 23: Forfeiture of Shares for Non-Payment
23.1 If a member fails to pay any call, or instalment of a call, on the day appointed for payment thereof, the Board may, at any time thereafter during such time as any part of the call or instalment remains unpaid, serve a notice on him requiring payment of so much of the call or instalment as is unpaid, together with any interest which may have accrued.

### Article 36: Exclusion of Chairman's Casting Vote & Multi-Tier Deadlock Protocol
36.1 **EXCLUSION OF CASTING VOTE**: In case of an equality of votes at any meeting of the Board of Directors, **the Chairman of the Board SHALL NOT have a second or casting vote**. Table F Regulation 68(ii) is hereby **expressly excluded and negated**. In the event of an equality of votes (a 1-1 split between the two Founder Directors), the proposed resolution shall not pass and shall constitute a **"Board Deadlock"**.  
36.2 **MULTI-TIER DEADLOCK RESOLUTION PROTOCOL**: If a Board Deadlock occurs on any matter necessary for the operation of the Company, the Founders agree to resolve the dispute through the following mandatory multi-tier escalation protocol:
- **(a) Tier 1 — Informal Founder Consultation (14 Days)**: Within fourteen (14) calendar days of the deadlocked Board meeting, Ranjith and Chandra Mohan shall convene in person or via secure video conference for good-faith, informal discussions to explore mutually acceptable commercial alternatives to resolve the impasse.
- **(b) Tier 2 — Conciliation and Professional Mediation (21 Days)**: If the dispute remains unresolved upon expiry of the 14-day consultation period, the Founders shall immediately refer the dispute to a mutually agreed neutral professional (**"Mediator"**), who shall be a practising Senior Advocate, a Fellow Chartered Accountant (FCA), or a Fellow Company Secretary (FCS) of not less than fifteen (15) years' standing. The Mediator shall hold conciliation sessions with both Founders within twenty-one (21) days of appointment to facilitate an amicable settlement. The fees of the Mediator shall be borne equally by the Company.
- **(c) Tier 3 — Binding Independent Expert Determination for Specific Matters**:
  - (i) If the deadlock relates to accounting interpretations, audited financial statements, tax treatments, or asset/share valuations, the matter shall be referred to an **Independent Registered Valuer** or Senior Chartered Accountant appointed pursuant to Article 19, whose written determination shall be final, conclusive, and binding on both Founders and the Company.
  - (ii) If the deadlock relates to technical architecture, software codebase, or artificial intelligence algorithms, the matter shall be referred to an independent agritech software expert mutually selected by the Founders.
- **(d) Tier 4 — Shareholder Referral & Contractual Exit**: If the deadlock involves a fundamental corporate policy matter and persists unresolved for more than forty-five (45) days following the deadlocked Board meeting:
  - (i) Either Founder may requisition an Extraordinary General Meeting under Section 100 to present the matter to the Members; and/or
  - (ii) Either Founder may trigger the buyout procedures, mediation clauses, or exit mechanisms established under any valid Shareholders' Agreement (SHA) executed between the Founders, or exercise the Right of First Refusal under Article 17 at Fair Market Value determined under Article 19.

## 1. MCA v3 e-AOA Filing Guidelines for Form INC-34

In the MCA v3 corporate filing architecture, electronic Articles of Association are submitted via **Form INC-34**.
The online form is pre-loaded with the 88 model regulations of **Schedule I, Table F**.

For every Table F regulation, the filing professional must select one of the following operations:
1. **Adopted**: Retain the standard Table F statutory text without modification.
2. **Modified**: Replace or adapt the Table F regulation with tailored company wording.
3. **Excluded / Not Applicable**: Mark the regulation as non-applicable to a private company limited by shares.
4. **Added**: Insert custom articles (e.g., Right of First Refusal, Permitted Transfers, Reserved Matters, Deadlock Resolution, IP Protection, Statutory Entrenchment).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Statutory Entrenchment under Section 5(3)**:
> In Form SPICe+ Part B (INC-32) and Form INC-34, select **YES** under *"Whether the Articles contain provisions for entrenchment?"*.
> Enter the details of entrenched articles: **Articles 16, 17, 36, 41, and 42**, specifying that they require **100% unanimous written consent of all members** to amend.

## 3. Practical Instructions for the Filing Professional

1. **MCA Portal Navigation**:
   - Access **SPICe+ Part B** $\rightarrow$ Form INC-34 (e-AOA).
   - In Table F regulation fields, copy and paste the corresponding custom articles for each *Modified* or *Added* entry as mapped above.
   - For *Excluded* regulations (specifically **Regulation 36** and **Regulation 68(ii)** regarding casting votes), select "Not Applicable" or clear the text box and indicate "Excluded by Article 28 and Article 36".
2. **Entrenchment Checkbox**:
   - Check the box for **Entrenchment Provisions (Section 5(3))**.
   - Input the entrenched article numbers: **Articles 16, 17, 36, 41, 42**.
   - Specify the condition: *"May be altered, amended, or deleted only with the unanimous (100%) written consent of all members of the Company."*
3. **Physical / Signed Attachment**:
   - In case MCA portal character limits prevent pasting the full text of all custom articles, attach the complete, signed PDF copy of the Articles of Association as an optional attachment to Form SPICe+ Part B.

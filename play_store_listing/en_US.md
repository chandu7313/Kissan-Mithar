**Kisan Mithar (క్రిసాన్ మిత్ర / किसान मित्र / ಕಿಸಾನ್ ಮಿತ್ರ)** is India's premier digital horticulture companion designed specifically for fruit growers, orchardists, and progressive farmers. 

Whether you are converting fallow land into high-yielding Mango, Guava, Sweet Lime, Custard Apple, Dragon Fruit, or Pomegranate plantations, Kisan Mithar guides you from soil preparation to market harvest!

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES

#### 1. 🗺️ Smart 3-Step Orchard Planning
- **Guided Camera Capture**: Upload multi-angle photos of your soil and topography.
- **Native GPS Mapping**: Accurately pin your land coordinates without complex paperwork.
- **Customized Agronomic Report**: Receive an 11-section personalized plan including plant spacing, high-density varieties, drip irrigation layout, fertilization schedules, and projected ROI over 5 years.

#### 2. 👨‍🌾 1-on-1 Certified Expert Consultations
- Book live voice, video, or chat consultations with leading agricultural scientists and state horticulture specialists.
- Get instant pest, fungal, and nutrient deficiency prescriptions directly on your phone.
- Set automatic reminders for spray timings and expert follow-ups.

#### 3. ⛅ Hyperlocal Farm Weather & Ag-Alerts
- Micro-climate hourly and 7-day rainfall forecasts based on your exact farm coordinates.
- Proactive disease and spraying advisories: "High humidity expected tomorrow — apply preventative spray."

#### 4. 💰 Government Subsidies & Schemes Guide
- Step-by-step assistance for MIDH (Mission for Integrated Development of Horticulture), PMKSY micro-irrigation subsidies, and state horticulture department incentives.

#### 5. 📡 100% Offline-First Architecture
- Seamlessly record voice notes, capture farm photos, and save drafts in remote farmlands with zero cellular connectivity. Automatically syncs when internet returns!
